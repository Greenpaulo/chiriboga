# R1.3 Nebula turn-planning hold

**Roadmap item:** R1.3 · **Depends on:** F4, R1.1 · **Sets:** elevation (Nebula Talent Management: Making Stars, 35057, once it has a definition)
**Read first:** `documentation/ai-principles.md`, `documentation/corp-ai/principles.md`, `documentation/corp-ai/specs/reactive-commitment-design.md`
**Verified against code:** 376f32c (2026-09-25)

## Goal
Let a Nebula Talent Management Corp keep an operation in HQ across the turn
boundary, so that when a successful Runner run on HQ or R&D flips the identity
back, the Corp can re-flip it on its next turn. The Nebula Fast Advance
precon's plan depends on this hold.

## Current behaviour
- Nebula Talent Management: Making Stars (35057) has **no card definition**
  (`documentation/card-status.md` lists it as "no definition"), so the item is
  parked until the card is implemented.
- Card text (from `carddata/carddata.json`):
  - Front: "When your action phase ends, if you played an operation this turn,
    gain 1 credit and flip this identity."
  - Flip side: "The first time each turn you play an operation, gain a click.
    When the Runner makes a successful run on HQ or R&D, flip this identity."
- So playing an operation does not flip the identity at once: the front side
  flips at the end of an action phase in which an operation was played. The
  flip side is lost when the Runner runs HQ or R&D successfully. The hold is
  "keep an operation in HQ so the next Corp turn can re-flip".
- No hook represents the hold; see R1.1 for the reservation record it will use.

## Design
- When the card is implemented, the identity declares `AIReservedValue`
  (R1.1) returning a `"turn"` record with `holds` naming one operation in HQ:
  the one with the lowest immediate value that the Corp can expect to afford
  next turn. The record is active while the identity is on its flip side, or
  will flip at the end of this action phase (front side, an operation already
  played this turn), and HQ holds an operation. On the front side before any
  operation is played this turn there is no hold: the Corp wants to play one.
- On the flip side, the first operation each turn gains a click, so playing
  one operation is usually free; the hold applies to the last operation in HQ
  (do not play it for marginal value), not to the first.
- The held operation is excluded from discretionary play and from discard or
  trash-from-HQ choices while an unheld card is available (R1.1's honouring
  rules); tactical overrides stay authoritative.
- `expiryCondition` is true when the held card leaves HQ or the identity is
  no longer Nebula.
- Behind the Corp AI option from R1.1, default off.

## Safety and information boundary
- Reads only public state and the Corp's own HQ; never predicts whether the
  Runner will run HQ or R&D from hidden cards.
- The hold covers one visible condition (the flip back), not a multi-turn
  plan.

## Test scenarios
1. Flip side, two operations in HQ, one already played this turn (click
   gained): the Corp does not play the second for marginal value, logging the
   hold reason.
2. The held operation is played when it completes a game-winning line this
   turn (for example, a fast-advance operation that scores the winning agenda).
3. Front side (flipped back), an operation in HQ: the Corp plays an operation
   this turn to re-flip; the hold does not block it.
4. Flip side, exactly one operation in HQ: held when its immediate value is
   below the record's value; played when above (values from the hook, asserted
   in the reason).
5. The Nebula hold and a Measured Response threshold hold (R1.1) in the same
   state are both honoured; when credits cannot cover both, the higher-value
   record is kept and the other is logged as displaced.
6. Scenarios 1 to 5 give identical decisions under two different Runner
   Grip/Stack substitutions of equal size.

## Acceptance gate
Gated. Deck pairs from F4's committed pool: Nebula Fast Advance as Corp
against at least three Runner precons, 200 games per pair, paired seeds.

- Improvement: `flippedTurnStarts` (share of Corp turns that begin with the
  identity on its flip side); the interval's lower bound is above zero.
- Guards: `winRate` no drop beyond 2 percentage points; `stallTurns` no
  increase beyond 0.2 per game; `unusedHandAtEnd` no increase beyond 0.5
  cards; `decisionLatencyMs` no increase beyond 10%.
- Collector added: `flippedTurnStarts`.

## Things to consider
- Unpark when Nebula Talent Management is defined and its flip is
  implemented; re-verify the card text and this design then.
- F4's deck pool must include Nebula Fast Advance as a Corp deck.
- R1's earlier scenario "a turn hold and a LEO mid-run hold do not interfere"
  cannot occur with real cards (both are identities); scenario 5 replaces it.
