# R1.1 Reservation hook and threshold hold

**Roadmap item:** R1.1 · **Depends on:** F4 · **Sets:** elevation (Measured Response); the existing `AIReserveCredits` declarations in systemgateway (Anoetic Void) and systemupdate2021 (Snare!) are reviewed for the new context argument
**Read first:** `documentation/ai-principles.md`, `documentation/corp-ai/principles.md`, `documentation/corp-ai/specs/reactive-commitment-design.md`
**Verified against code:** 376f32c (2026-09-25)

## Goal
Add the reservation hook and record from the design note, and make its first
declaration a persistent-threshold hold: once Measured Response can soon be
played, the Corp keeps the card and its play cost instead of spending the
credits on a marginal action. R1.2 (LEO, mid-run) and R1.3 (Nebula,
turn-planning) build on this item.

## Current behaviour
- **Measured Response** (`sets/elevation.js`, Weyland Black Ops operation,
  play cost 5): playable only when `max(AgendaPoints(corp),
  AgendaPoints(runner)) >= 4` and its `successfulRunLastTurn` flag is set. The
  flag is set by its `responseOnRunSuccessful` and cleared by its
  `responseOnRunnerTurnBegins` (both `availableWhenInactive`, so they work from
  HQ). It does 4 meat damage unless the Runner pays 8 credits. Its only AI hook
  is `AIPriority`; nothing keeps 5 credits for it, so the Corp can reach the
  turn after a successful run unable to afford it.
- **Credit reservation already exists.** `AIReserveCredits(server)` lets a
  card declare credits it expects to spend after rez. `_sufficientEconomy()`
  sums it (through `_reserveCreditsForCard()`) over **installed cards only**,
  on top of the top-three rez costs, and caps the total at 12 when the Corp has
  more credits than the Runner. `_iceWorthRezzing()` also asks the attacked
  server's root cards and, for a central, its `cards` (so HQ hand cards when
  the server is HQ) and treats each declaration as an infinitely valuable
  reserve; it separately reserves rez credits for a higher-value server's ICE
  (the cross-server rez reserve). Declarations today: Anoetic Void
  (systemgateway, installed), Snare! (systemupdate2021, installed or in
  HQ/R&D), and Aggressive Secretary and Project Junebug in the deprecated
  coreset.
- `_sufficientEconomy()` only steers: when false, `Phase_Main` falls back to
  `_bestMainPhaseEconomyOption()`, but only after the install plan has found
  nothing, so it does not stop a paid install or advance chosen earlier.
- No hook or record represents holding a card or ability for a future
  condition. See [architecture.md: not yet modelled, holding and trigger ordering](../architecture.md#not-yet-modelled-holding-and-trigger-ordering).

## Design
Contracts are in the design note ("Proposed architecture"); this item builds
them and the first declaration.

- **Hook.** `card.AIReservedValue(context)` returns `null` or a record
  `{value, timing, reason, expiryCondition, holds?}` (design note). Identity
  cards use the same hook; there is no separate `AIReservedAbilityValue`.
- **Record store.** `corp.AI._reservations` maps each declaring card to
  `{record, sinceTurn}`. It is refreshed once per Corp `Choice` that consults
  it and at Corp turn start in `_prepareProtectionPrioritiesForCorpTurn()`.
  Refresh order: drop records whose card has left HQ, the board or the
  identity slot; evaluate each surviving record's `expiryCondition` and drop it
  (logging `reservation expired: <reason>`) when true; then ask each candidate
  card's hook again and drop records that come back `null`. `sinceTurn` is kept
  when the same card returns a record again, for telemetry.
- **Credits: extend `AIReserveCredits`, no second mechanism.** The signature
  becomes `AIReserveCredits(server, context)` with `context.purpose` of
  `"economy"` (from `_sufficientEconomy()`) or `"rez"` (from
  `_iceWorthRezzing()`); existing declarations ignore the argument and are
  unchanged. `_sufficientEconomy()` additionally sums declarations from HQ
  cards, passing `corp.HQ` as `server`, **only for cards with an active
  reservation record**, so Snare! in HQ is not newly counted. A card that holds
  credits declares both hooks and derives `AIReserveCredits` from its own
  `AIReservedValue`, so the two cannot disagree. `AIReservedValue` never
  reserves credits by itself.
- **Honouring the hold.** A reserved card is not chosen by
  `_bestDiscardOption()` (or any discard/trash-from-HQ choice) while an
  unreserved card is available. Credits reserved by HQ cards are subtracted
  from the credits that discretionary spends may use, through one helper shared
  by the spend sites (optional ICE installs with an install cost, advancing a
  card that cannot score this turn, non-economy operations). The Resolution
  lists every site that honours it.
- **Tactical overrides.** The reservation is ignored by game-winning scores,
  `_criticalBreachDefenseAction()`, `_emergencyProtectionRecovery()`, rezzes
  that `_icePreventsGameWinningBreach()` approves, and forced single-option
  decisions. A reservation never stops the reserved card itself from being
  played.
- **Measured Response declaration.** In HQ, with threat level at least 4, it
  returns a `"threshold"` record (reason names the threat level and play cost)
  whose value is bounded by public Runner state (credits against the 8-credit
  payment; grip size against 4 meat damage), clamped as the design note says.
  `expiryCondition` is true once the card is no longer in HQ. Its
  `AIReserveCredits` returns its play cost for `"economy"` while its record is
  active and 0 for `"rez"`, so it never makes `_iceWorthRezzing()` decline an
  HQ rez. Whether to start holding at threat level 3 is a gate option, off by
  default.
- **Not scored yet.** Until I7.2 builds a scored candidate model for
  main-phase actions, a reservation is a constraint (exclusion and credit
  floor), not a scored alternative. I7.2 computes `immediateValueIfSpentNow`
  against the record's `value`.
- The whole change sits behind one Corp AI option, default off.

## Safety and information boundary
- Hooks read only public state and the Corp's own cards; they never read the
  Runner's Grip or Stack contents (Grip size is public), or Corp R&D order.
- Only HQ cards with an active record contribute hand reservations; R&D and
  Archives cards never do.
- Tactical overrides above stay authoritative in every case.

## Test scenarios
1. Threat level 3, Measured Response in HQ: no record, and `_sufficientEconomy()`
   returns the same result as with the option off.
2. Threat level 4, Measured Response in HQ, Corp at 7 credits, flag not set:
   the record exists; offered an optional 3-credit ICE install on a server
   `_evaluateServerSecurity()` reports secure, the Corp declines it and takes
   another action, logging the reservation reason.
3. As 2, but the spend is tactically authoritative (a rez that prevents a
   game-winning breach, or a scoring action that wins): the spend happens and
   the log names the override.
4. Expiry: the record survives the Corp turn ending with the flag unset (the
   Runner has not yet run successfully) and is re-established at the next Corp
   turn start; it is dropped with `reservation expired` once Measured Response
   is played, discarded, or trashed from HQ by Anoetic Void. With the flag set,
   threat level 4 and 5 credits, the reservation does not stop the card being
   played.
5. At a hand-size discard, `_bestDiscardOption()` keeps Measured Response while
   an unreserved card is available.
6. During a run on HQ, Measured Response's reservation never makes
   `_iceWorthRezzing()` decline an HQ ICE rez (`"rez"` purpose returns 0).
7. With the option on and no card declaring `AIReservedValue`, decisions are
   identical to the option off (decision snapshots).
8. A hook returning a non-finite value or no reason is ignored, with a logged
   reason.
9. Scenarios 1 to 6 give identical decisions under two different Runner
   Grip/Stack substitutions of equal size.

## Acceptance gate
Gated (see `documentation/ai-planning.md`, "Acceptance gates"). Deck pairs from
F4's committed pool: Zwicky Supermodernism as Corp against at least three
Runner precons, plus two control Corp decks without a declaring card; 200
games per pair, paired seeds.

- Improvement: `reservedPlayMissedTurns` per game on the Zwicky pairs; the
  interval of the reduction has its lower bound above zero.
- Guards on the Zwicky pairs: `winRate` no drop beyond 2 percentage points;
  `stallTurns` no increase beyond 0.2 per game; `unusedCreditsAtEnd` no
  increase beyond 2 credits; `unusedHandAtEnd` no increase beyond 0.5 cards;
  `decisionLatencyMs` no increase beyond 10%.
- Control pairs: every metric identical to baseline.

Collectors added by this item through F4's collector extension point
(`stallTurns` and `unusedCreditsAtEnd` only if I0 has not already added them,
with these definitions):
`stallTurns` (Corp turns where every click went to the basic credit action or
was unused although the Corp began the turn able to afford a legal install or
play), `unusedCreditsAtEnd`, `unusedHandAtEnd` (Corp HQ size at game end),
`reservedPlayMissedTurns` (Corp turns that began with a reserved card legally
playable but unaffordable).

## Things to consider
- F4's deck pool must include Zwicky Supermodernism (and, for R1.2, LEO
  Glacier) as Corp decks. If it does not, adding them is this item's first gate
  step.
- The 12-credit cap in `_sufficientEconomy()` can absorb a 5-credit hand
  reservation when installed rez costs are high; decide whether hand
  reservations sit outside the cap and record why.
- Measured Response also appears in the Glyph of Warding, Pork Chops and PT
  Untaian precons; they can serve as extra gate pairs.
- A hold never grows into multi-turn win-line planning (design note non-goal).
