# L8.8 Marginal deception value

**Roadmap item:** L8.8 · **Depends on:** I2, F2, F4, F11, L8.4, L8.7 · **Sets:** playable sets (`documentation/card-sets.md`)
**Read first:** `documentation/ai-principles.md`, `documentation/corp-ai/principles.md`, [install design](install-decisions-design.md), [I2](I2-ice-selection-by-marginal-security.md), [deception gate reconciliation](../deception-gates.md)
**Verified against code:** e33a91b (2026-10-12)

## Goal

Replace the install design's bounded profile-based `deceptionValue` placeholder
with an explicitly justified marginal value of preserving a shared deceptive
posture. Separate that value from the private mechanical payoff of a trap.
First adopt the shared component in I2's ordinary ICE ranking through a new,
separately gated option. I3, I5 and I7 may subsequently consume the same
contract through their own scoped gates; this item does not migrate them all.

This is a sibling of L8.2 (legibility), not a prerequisite for I2. I2 keeps
its current symmetric placeholder and substitution invariant until this
follow-up is independently adopted. Dependencies ensure the consumer, paired
metric support, posture epochs and punishment-hook audit are available.
L8.5 feedback and L8.6 human telemetry are optional evidence sources, not
required dependencies or assumed implementations. L8.2 ordering must be
recorded before evaluation, with matching effective options in both arms.

## Current behaviour

- `_remoteDeceptionProfile(card)` supplies a shared distribution of target ICE
  count, opening advancement and delay for agendas and eligible traps.
  `_deceptionInstallDistance(card, server)` measures ICE-count distance, not
  expected payoff. Profiles retain their lifetime shape; active postures use
  bounded epochs when `postureEpochs` is on (currently the default).
- `_accessPunishmentSeverity(server)` reads the strongest finite non-negative
  `AIPunishesAccess(server)` severity among hidden installed remote roots.
  `_calculateBaitFrequency(server)` uses severity to set bait frequency.
  Neither function estimates access probability or credit-equivalent payoff.
- [The hook contract](../../ai.md#59-access-punishment--aipunishesaccess) reports
  a planning weight, not expected damage or guaranteed unpreventable harm.
  Urtica Cipher reports `2 + advancement`; another current hook reports
  severity 4 only when the Corp can pay 4 credits for its effect. These values
  cannot be interpreted directly as Corp credits or win probabilities.
- The install design separately reserves `trapValue` (0 to 4), `deceptionValue`
  (0 to 1), `futureValue` (0 to 1) and `securityGain` (0 to 8). These are planned
  score contracts, not evidence that all consumers already implement them.
  `_deceptionUpside()` does not exist at this verification stamp.
- I2's updated spec already gives active agenda/trap postures a common
  consequence weight and requires equivalent ordinary ICE decisions with
  equivalent mechanics, costs and reserves. The claim that missing deception
  value causes the tell remains a hypothesis; a new bonus is not a substitute
  for checking admission, allocation and actual observable defenses.

See [current architecture](../architecture.md#baits-bluffs-and-deterrence).
Before raising this spec, reverify these claims and the adopted I2 consumer.
The formula and response model are unresolved research questions, so this
proposal is not implementation-ready. Do not defer their resolution to an
implementation plan or raise this spec unchanged.

## Design

### Feasibility before raising or implementing

Resolve these steps in order while the item remains `proposed`:

1. **Decision-gap screen first.** On adopted I2, capture real candidate records
   for ordinary band-1 decisions, including eligible near-ties and the existing
   placeholder. Compute each base score excluding `deceptionValue`, with all
   other components fixed and any proposed overlap removal recorded separately.
   A new [0, 1] component can change the relative score of two candidates by
   at most 1: a base gap greater than 1 cannot be reversed; a gap of exactly 1
   can only tie, so inspect the actual tie-break. If comparing old and new
   total rankings, account for replacing the old [0, 1] placeholder rather
   than adding another bonus: the pairwise change can be as large as 2.
   Being within those bounds establishes possibility, not a realizable delta.
   Record the actual hypothetical before/after levels needed for one useful
   reversal, eligibility, costs, reasons and final choice. If representative
   decisions provide no useful realizable gap, park or rescope before building
   the helper. A synthetic near-tie alone does not establish practical need.
2. **Choose the claim.** This item's target remains an evaluated marginal
   payoff. A profile-distance preference is not an acceptable substitute for
   that claim, even if it passes a gate. If only a bounded preference is
   supportable, park this attempt or explicitly rescope it as a preference
   item with revised goal, formula and acceptance criteria before raising it.
   Do not silently relabel the implementation at hand-off.
3. **Finish the model in this spec.** Specify the level formula, horizon,
   response counterfactuals, evidence sources, conversion into Corp-credit
   units, uncertainty handling and domain of validity. Show why its shared
   inputs preserve substitution and why its attainable deltas can address the
   screened decision gap. Freeze those details before implementation or
   candidate gate results. If no defensible model exists, park the item.

At e33a91b, I2 remains proposed and its marginal scorer is not implemented;
the score bounds permit near-tie changes algebraically, but the empirical
screen above cannot yet be completed against an adopted scorer. Do not
report a demonstrated decision gap or working valuation from that bound.

[L8.7's linked ticket](../../backlog/feature-layer-8-card-set-audit-and-set-adoption.md)
is the access/tag-punishment hook audit. At this verification stamp it is
`ready`, has no dependencies and is not `done`. It is scheduled ahead of
L8.8 by the recorded dependency; recheck completion before implementation.
Do not treat the current three access-hook declarers as a completed audit.

### Separate the two outputs

Propose a read-only `_deceptionUpside(server, card, context)` helper, or an
equivalent API, with named outputs and evidence status rather than one scalar:

- `sharedConcealmentValue`: a level in [0, 1] credit-equivalents, based only on
  the observable shape, authoritative shared profile/posture, public history
  and an explicit opponent-response assumption. Conditional on equivalent
  mechanics, costs and reserves, changing hidden root type or agenda points
  must not change this level or its ordinary ICE consumer.
- `mechanicalPayoff`: private diagnostic breakdown of punishment severity,
  trigger affordability, known prevention/avoidance, estimated access
  probability and costs. Unknown terms remain unknown, not invented zeroes
  or certain outcomes. Raw severity is not a credit-equivalent payoff.
  Only a separately justified conversion may supply I5's bounded `trapValue`.
- `assumptions` / `evidenceStatus`: distinguish known mechanics, an explicit
  modeled response and an empirically calibrated response. Record unsupported
  inputs and why a component contributes no scored value.

A card argument allows Corp-known mechanics in the private breakdown; it
must not silently enter the shared value. Equal units do not imply equal
agenda/trap behavior. Do not copy the actual trap's severity into the shared
score, and do not assign agendas a fictional trap payload for parity.
A shared belief model, if proposed, needs a separately justified information
source and identical application to equivalent public shapes.

### Score the candidate's change

Evaluate before and after each proposed action, using F2/I1's guarded
hypothetical machinery. The context names the action, horizon, projected
costs and authoritative posture captured before hypothetical evaluation.
Return `sharedDelta = after.sharedConcealmentValue - before.sharedConcealmentValue`.
A constant level gives zero marginal value; it is not a bonus for every ICE.

The existing `deceptionValue` score contract stays in [0, 1]. For initial
adoption, only `max(0, sharedDelta)` may enter that component. Record negative
deltas diagnostically; existing posture eligibility/envelopes remain
responsible for constraints. Introducing a scored deception penalty requires
an explicit shared-design range change and separate scope/gate evidence.
Do not hide a negative delta in another score component. This initial policy
is deliberately one-sided: it rewards improvements in shared posture value
but cannot numerically penalize breaking a posture. Existing envelopes
constrain layer count, not every degradation of concealment. Test an eligible
within-envelope degradation explicitly and record this limitation on adoption;
do not describe positive-only scoring as full posture preservation.

The feasibility stage must resolve the formula, horizon, conversion and
response model in this spec before it is raised. An implementation plan then
implements that defined contract rather than supplying its core valuation.
Unsupported speculative upside contributes zero. Never infer likelihood
from severity alone.

### Distinguish mechanisms and prevent double counting

Agenda bluffing can discourage a profitable run; trap baiting can attract a
punishing run. Those are different response counterfactuals. Runner clicks
and credits spent on a run are neither automatically equal to Corp credits
nor automatically additional value attributable to deception.

Assign each modeled effect one owner:

| Effect | Scored owner / restriction |
| --- | --- |
| Mandatory ICE costs, lockout and bypass | `securityGain`; never repeated as deception tempo |
| Actual access punishment, including damage/tag/credit-loss riders | I5 `trapValue`; private breakdown only in this item's ICE adoption |
| Concealment attributable to the candidate's shared public posture | Marginal `deceptionValue`; equal on equivalent postured agenda/trap boards |
| Delayed rez capacity or speculative ICE usefulness | `futureValue`; remove any overlapping bluff contribution when adopting this helper |
| Scoring progress, displaced roles, paid costs and reserves | Existing owning components; no duplicate deception reward |

Optional punishment and deterrence never manufacture deterministic security.
A possible flatline must not become a certain win from severity: respect
public Grip size, known prevention and access/avoidance uncertainty without
reading hidden cards. This item does not introduce a new tactical win oracle.

## Safety and information boundary

- Use public Runner information and Corp-known mechanics only; never read
  hidden Runner identities, decklists or private AI/run-calculator state.
- Preserve I2's full ordinary-ranking substitution invariant: components,
  eligibility, reserves, allocation, tie-breaks and final choice. Genuine
  mechanical/cost differences and authoritative safety branches remain separate.
- Forced/game-winning actions and breach-loss safety outrank all deception
  values. Existing posture caps and epoch decisions remain authoritative.
- Evaluation must not reroll or advance a posture epoch, record public history,
  or leak hypothetical state. Repeated probes give identical outputs.
- Initial consumer option `marginalDeceptionValue` defaults off. With it off,
  decisions and seeded traces match the adopted I2 control.

## Test scenarios

1. Swap agenda/trap roots and vary agenda points on otherwise equivalent active
   postures. Shared levels, deltas, scored components and final ICE decisions
   match; only private payoff diagnostics differ.
2. Two ICE candidates leaving the same shared value receive no spurious
   differentiation. A constant server value adds no marginal bonus.
3. A candidate improving the modeled shared shape earns a bounded positive
   delta; a worsening candidate records a negative diagnostic delta without
   violating the current [0, 1] score contract.
4. Disabled or unaffordable punishment reports its actual hook result; missing
   access/response estimates stay unknown. Urtica damage is not assumed lethal
   or unpreventable, and severity is not converted directly to credits.
5. Swap hidden Runner Grip/Stack contents, or throw on private-state reads:
   every output and decision stays unchanged.
6. Winning-breach safety and forced/winning scoring choices remain authoritative,
   including a case where the shared estimate favors a lighter posture.
7. Repeat and reorder hypothetical probes: no rolls, telemetry observations,
   epoch transitions or persistent state changes. A real epoch reevaluation
   changes the authoritative input used by subsequent planning.
8. Break cost, punishment, future bluff value and run tempo each contribute once;
   show counterexamples where summing overlapping estimates would change choice.
9. Option off preserves adopted I2 decisions; option on changes at least one
   justified ordinary ICE decision rather than only its logs.
10. Revealed roots, neutral postures, multiple roots and genuinely unequal
    mechanics/reserves follow explicit eligibility rules, with no accidental
    agenda/trap priority outside the documented safety branch.

## Acceptance gate

Strategic change: F4 paired-seed gate, committed deck pool, 200 games per deck
pair initially, bootstrap 95% intervals. Control uses adopted I2 and the option
off; candidate differs only by `marginalDeceptionValue=true`. Record effective
L8.2 and all prerequisite options, and refresh both reports after any
decision-changing prerequisite lands.

Preregister an improvement in `winRate` (paired 95% improvement interval
above zero), guards on `pointsScored` and `pointsStolen` of 0.2 points,
`gameLength` of 1.5, `corpInsolventTurns` of 0.5 and
`strandedUnrezzedIceCost.credits` of 1, plus the fresh same-machine +25%
mean-latency guard. Declare better directions explicitly.

Reuse the existing posture-roll collector and F12's realized-count collector
(as consumed by I2) with F11's paired
batch guards. Revalidate sample coverage and control-only precision; adopt
only preregistered, justified margins, never assume I2's provisional 0.02 rho
is calibrated. Record intervals, sample counts and root/count distributions.
`installOutcomes.trapTriggers` remains diagnostic, not proof of concealment.
Do not invent a runnable command until metric paths, collectors, margins and
sample sizes are verified and frozen before candidate results. Thin samples
require larger cohorts or separately gated real-game-derived starts; missing
root classes or no changed games do not pass.

F4 can observe punishment and wasted resources even though the Runner AI does
not learn tells. It cannot establish the extra benefit against an opponent
learning those tells. Label that benefit unvalidated; human evidence belongs
to L8.6 or a separately specified response-model experiment. A passing F4 gate
establishes benefit/safety against its control, not successful human deception
or full multivariable unpredictability. Passing the realized-count guards
means only that measured count-based tells did not regress beyond their frozen
margins; it does not show the helper concealed anything, reduced an existing
tell, or addressed the hypothesized cause. State this in the Resolution even
when all guards pass. Do not fit response assumptions to
candidate gate outcomes. Gate failure follows `ai-planning.md`: not adopted,
remove the failed policy branch, retain useful passive evidence and park or
rescope from that evidence.
