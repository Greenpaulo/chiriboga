### What “Specific case” actually does

[Verified] In `ai_corp.js`, `_rankedInstallOptionsCore()` lines 6046–6052 chooses
`strongestEmptyRemote` from `_emptyProtectedRemotes()`, or leaves it null.
The “Specific case” clause at lines 6339–6351 checks for Snare!, that the Runner
cannot look at it, and more than three usable Corp credits; it then appends an
install into that strongest remote. Null means a **new remote**.
`_emptyProtectedRemotes()` (line 4806 onwards) requires existing ICE, allows
upgrades/replaceable obsolete bluffs, and sorts by `_protectionScore()`.
Thus protection is considered when room exists. **There is no deliberate
bare-server bait preference in this clause.** Its new-remote fallback permits a
bare trap without applying the scoring-server admission used for HVTs.
This is a candidate-admission difference, not evidence that a protected candidate
lost to a bare one on the scoring comparator.

The HVT generator (lines 6162–6174) uses `_scoringServers()`. The comparator's
named components remain `advancementWindowDistance = abs(advancementLimit -
scoringWindow)` and `weightedProfileDistance = 0.75 * profileDistance` (lines
6237–6277). The “Specific case” append happens after that sort. Do not route
Snare! through advancement ranking merely to imitate agendas: its advancement
inputs need not be finite or meaningful.

### Sample size for a 0.02 absolute win-rate drop

A drop of 0.02 means two percentage points (for example 50% to 48%). Planning
assumptions here are a **single** comparison, two-sided 5% significance, 80%
power, matched seed/deck pairs, independent game pairs and a normal large-sample
approximation. The actual F4 gate uses a paired bootstrap rather than McNemar's
test; these are planning estimates, not exact power guarantees for its gate.

Let `q` be the fraction of paired games where the Corp-win indicator differs
between policy off and on (both directions combined), and `d=0.02`. With
`zA=1.95996398454`, `zPower=0.84162123357`, approximate pairs needed are:
`ceil(((zA*sqrt(q) + zPower*sqrt(q-d*d))/d)^2)`.
This is the large-sample paired-proportions planning formula, with alpha/2 for
two-sided significance; the neglected opposite tail is negligible at 80% power.
See [Stata's paired-proportions methods, equations 2–3](https://www.stata.com/manuals/pss-2powerpairedproportions.pdf).
`winRatePairs()` in the calibration tool reproduces the calculation, including
Stata's published 82-pair example as a regression test.

| Corp-win outcome discordance q | Approximate pairs required | Seeds per each of 7 deck pairs | Balanced pairs / total game plays |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 5% | 979 | 140 | 980 / 1,960 |
| 10% | 1,960 | 280 | 1,960 / 3,920 |
| 20% | 3,923 | 561 | 3,927 / 7,854 |
| 50% | 9,809 | 1,402 | 9,814 / 19,628 |
| 100% (conservative upper scenario) | 19,620 | 2,803 | 19,621 / 39,242 |

At q=10%, 90% power instead needs approximately 2,623 pairs. Without an assumed
pairing benefit, independent 50%-versus-48% arms need about 9,806 games **per
arm** (19,612 total) at 80% power, via the ordinary two-proportion approximation.
Paired and independent sample units must not be confused.

The proposed 400 seeds × 7 pairs gives **2,800 matched pairs / 5,600 plays**.
It reaches approximately 80% power for this effect only if q is at most about
14.3% (at q=10% power is about 92%; q=20%, about 66%; q=50%, about 32%). Policy
identity controls have q=0 by construction and cannot estimate repair-induced
q. A pilot/sample-size rule would need to be specified before candidate results;
do not choose a favorable q or extend an adoption run after seeing its result.
These numbers describe this fixed seven-deck mixture, not all possible decks
or human opponents.

**Detection versus guard:** the existing `winRate=0.02` guard asks whether its
confidence interval rules out losses beyond two points; it is not a test that
must first detect a loss versus zero. It can fail from uncertainty even without
a significant loss. A true loss exactly at the two-point boundary should almost
never pass the guard (roughly 2.5% asymptotically for its 95% interval). Thus a
2,800-pair gate is neither automatic protection from every two-point loss nor an
80%-powered detector regardless of discordance. No win-rate margin is changed
by this planning analysis.

## Design

### Passive attribution

Extend F12's delivered observations with commitment IDs and records of the
values already used by actual decisions. At a root install, capture eligibility,
selected and alternative destinations, pre-install ICE counts, actual sort rank,
advancement limit, scoring window, protection score, existing profile distance,
posture/safety state and choice reason. Capture later ICE installs/removals,
posture transitions and each Runner-turn snapshot. Represent Infinity explicitly
in exported data. Label evaluator-derived security as Corp-known assessment,
not as information visible to the Runner about unrezzed ICE.

Instrument existing evaluation results; never re-sort options or call a posture/
profile helper to obtain a diagnostic that would roll or cache new state. An
uncomputed input is `unknown`, not a reason to evaluate a new decision path.
Record start-board commitments with left-censoring metadata; unknown original
placement is not invented. Root classes are harness-only truth labels, defined
by agenda type or Ambush/access-punishment declarations, not card-title lists.

Report both the existing snapshot statistic and commitment-level distributions:

- ICE present when each root was installed;
- subsequent ICE added/removed during comparable root-age windows;
- postured/unpostured, single/multi-root, revealed/unrevealed and safety cases;
- agenda/trap commitment counts, usable games and lifecycle endpoints (score,
  steal, overwrite, trash, game end).

Agendas can leave through scoring while traps persist. Repeated snapshots
therefore measure exposure, not independent placements. Cluster uncertainty
within games; report age-stratified and one-observation-per-commitment results
so survival differences are visible. Multi-root observations share one defense
and must not be treated as independent servers. Revealed roots are diagnostic,
not hidden-class tells. Keep safety overrides in headline coverage and identify
rather than discard them.

### Attribution and disposition

Run control-only `--quick`, then 200 seeds per committed deck pair with the
current adopted options recorded. Separate these hypotheses:

1. Candidate availability/eligibility or placement already differs at install.
2. Class-dependent posture eligibility/rates change capped-server shares.
3. Later unpostured protection decisions create or widen the gap.
4. Root survival, draws, multi-root mechanics or revealed cards explain residue.

Use temporal records to distinguish an observed association from a mechanism
actually exercised in a decision. Observational splits need not produce additive
causal percentages. If evidence is ambiguous, state what experiment would resolve
it rather than claiming an exact attribution. Controlled diagnostic experiments
may proceed after their hypothesis, options and measures are recorded in this
ticket, before examining their candidate results.

### Repair in the same ticket

Record an attribution table, then choose the smallest supported change to
placement, posture eligibility or later ICE allocation. Document its root cause,
scope, named score components and deterministic acceptance scenarios here before
implementation. Follow the ordinary implement-ticket planning rules for risky
changes; no second ticket is required. Keep the repair behind default-off
`observableDefenseParity`. If the repair belongs to placement, coordinate its
boundary with I3 so the same rule is not implemented twice.

Preserve tactical safety and equivalent agenda/trap active-posture ranking.
Do not equalise raw counts by wasting ICE or removing necessary agenda defense.
Do not condition decisions on harness-only labels or private Runner information.
Sampled agenda requirements do not prove placement parity, public-variable
posture rates can introduce new tells, and restricting postures to single-root servers would not
erase multi-root observations. Attribution determines the design, not exemption
from the improvement gate.

If there is no worthwhile repair or evidence remains insufficient, record that
result in this ticket and leave the policy unadopted. Do not claim tell reduction
or silently complete the implementation objective from observation alone.
