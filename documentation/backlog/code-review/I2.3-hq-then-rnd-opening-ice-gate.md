## Current behaviour

- `iceMarginalSecurity` defaults to true. `_marginalIceInstallOptions()` ranks
  admitted ICE/server pairs by affordability group, marginal score, then walk
  order; it has no HQ-first/R&D-second requirement. `_rankedInstallOptionsCore()`
  consumes that list and also offers a new-server ICE fallback.
- `_iceInstallOptions()` preserves card suitability and affordability lists.
  A card can be in the low-priority list despite sufficient printed rez funds
  because `AIWorthwhileIce` rejects its present suitability (for example Magnet).
- `_recordProtectionInstall()` records a preferred protection action through
  `_returnPreference()`, before completed installation. It is not an oracle
  for the first two completed ICE installs and does not cover every install.
- I2.2 reproduced later R&D protection in 700 paired diagnostic games:
  conditional first R&D ICE turn 1.280 → 2.026, never protected 4 → 40.

  favourable replay examples; those observations do not prove A is better.

  and first turn, with zero meaning none; its `scanIce()`/`WeakSet` source misses
  reinstallations of the same object (re-grounded at `15e012e`). Separate completion-based
  reporting is required by this experiment; preserve the existing historical metric. `centralStolen.points` is exactly
  HQ plus R&D theft; it excludes Archives. The adoption checks below use
  existing metrics; the new diagnostic collector is a required setup task.

## Design

**Classification:** strategic preference between legal choices, requiring a
default-off Corp option `openingHqThenRdIce` and an F4 adoption gate.
`iceMarginalSecurity=true` remains explicit in **both** arms. No calibration
changes, central score bonuses, other I2 changes or new card-specific rules.

1. Keep a per-game ordinal of **completed Corp ICE installations**. Before
   install 1, restrict voluntary ICE destinations to HQ; before install 2,
   restrict them to R&D. Starting from install 3, return to B's destination
   policy. Rank eligible cards for the required destination using unchanged
   I2 affordability groups and marginal scores. This is a hard destination
   constraint, not a bonus that a sufficiently attractive remote can beat.
2. Apply it consistently to the main protection list, fallback ICE list,
   install selection, and other AI-controlled ICE destination choices,
   including card-granted installs. Prevent a later fallback or direct route
   from silently installing the opening ICE on a remote or doubling HQ.
   Normal non-ICE choices, drawing, economy and action ordering stay intact;
   this ticket does not force ICE to be the first two **actions** or add draw
   pressure to find a second ICE. A one-ICE hand may still leave R&D bare.
3. Keep engine legality, card-owned suitability and existing affordability
   groups. If no ICE can be offered at the required destination, do not
   substitute another voluntary ICE destination; take an otherwise available
   non-ICE action or decline an optional install. A high remote score, low
   central stake, poor economy or bluff posture is not an escape from A.
4. Shared principle 4 still governs forced actions and immediate game-winning
   or game-losing tactics. A rules-forced install with no legal required-central
   destination, or an immediate outcome override, must be identified and
   recorded as an explicit exception, never hidden as normal compliance.
   The literal completed-install ordinal still advances for such an install;
   do not turn this experiment into a renewable central-coverage quota.
   Report exception counts and replay each distinct reason. Do not widen
   this exception to ordinary strategic urgency.
5. Advance the ordinal once, at actual installation completion, across all
   routes. Ranking, hypotheticals, selecting a preference, cancelled installs,
   moving installed ICE and rez events do not advance it. Trashing/bouncing
   an installed ICE does not undo a completed install; reinstalling that card
   is another completed install. Reset only for a new game. Determine the
   completion integration point and every affected caller during implementation
   validation; `_recordProtectionInstall()` alone is insufficient.
6. Log ordinal, required/actual destination, candidate group/score and the
   rule or exception that decided it. Repeated planning and observation must
   preserve live state and RNG. Option-off choices and logs retain their
   existing behaviour; existing green expectations are unchanged.

7. Add passive `openingCentralCoverage` measurements. At actual Corp turn-end
   boundaries 1 and 2, snapshot the **current** HQ/R&D ICE counts, not just
   whether an installation happened earlier. Extend the harness's Corp
   `turnEnd` observation payload with those counts; do not add a decision
   consumer. A collector reports `turn1.observed`, `turn1.both`,
   `turn2.observed`, `turn2.both`, where `both` is 1 only when at least one
   ICE is currently installed on **each** central at that boundary. Missing
   boundaries are unobserved, not successes or ordinary failures. Emit no
   extra AI evaluations, card hooks or random calls. These are planned
   measurements; neither the payload extension nor collector exists yet.
8. Hold the current mulligan policy and main-phase draw/economy ordering
   identical in A and B. Record the initially offered hand's ICE count,
   keep/mulligan choice, post-mulligan hand's ICE count and printed rez-cost
   distribution, then the ICE available after the compulsory first draw.
   Counts use Corp-known information; affordability/suitability must be
   distinguished from printed card count and from the funds needed to rez
   two ICE together. Add these as passive `openingCentralCoverage` samples.
   Opening mulligan strata are defined before A/B diverge, so record matched
   cohorts of 0, 1 and 2+ opening ICE and paired boundary outcomes within each.
   Do not retune mulligans to make A work during its placement gate.
