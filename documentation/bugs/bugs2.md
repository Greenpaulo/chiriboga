1. [FIXED] When accessing all cards in archives, I didnt get a chance to stop and trash any with Carnivore.

   **Resolved — 21 Sept:** This is correct rules behavior rather than an **Access All** bug. Every card in the reported breach was already in Archives, and Comprehensive Rules 7.1.5b forbids trashing a card in the Corp's discard pile with either the basic trash ability or another mid-access ability such as Carnivore. `CheckTrash()` already enforces that rule, so there was no legal choice for **Access All** to pause on. Added regression coverage confirming that Carnivore is unavailable for cards in the Archives pile but remains available for a trashable accessed card elsewhere. See `documentation/bugs/code-review/carnivore-access-all-archives.md`.
